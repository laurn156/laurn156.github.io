## Overview
This project involved the design and development of a bat-inspired Unmanned 
Aerial System (UAS) intended to one day use sonar echolocation to navigate 
spaces and avoid obstacles. Modeled after the Grey Long-Eared Bat, the drone 
was designed with future sensor integration in mind — providing a complete 
mechanical foundation for autonomous navigation to be built upon.

## What We Built
- Custom 3D-printed components designed in SOLIDWORKS: bat head attachment, 
  ESC housing, battery box, and mounting brackets
- Conducted FEA structural analysis across two design iterations to identify 
  stress points and refine geometry
- Clearance analysis and scaled prototypes to validate part tolerances 
  to within 0.3mm
- Full system weight breakdown across frame, electronics, propulsion, 
  and printed parts

## Design Highlights
- **Frame:** Stacked carbon fiber plates with four extending booms
- **Bat head:** Designed to house a forward-facing speaker and two 
  omnidirectional microphones for future sonar integration
- **3D-printed parts:** Entirely designed in SOLIDWORKS, optimized for 
  weight and modularity
- **Total estimated mass:** ~4,194g
